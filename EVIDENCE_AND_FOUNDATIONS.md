# Evidence and Foundations — RAP v4.0

The external literature supports comparison, scope control, and benchmark selection. It does not replace the RAP evidence hierarchy, derive the finite kernel, or upgrade a RAP-specific mapping. UIDT denotes the historical lineage rather than the active ontology.

The guide foregrounds finite real spectral triples and algebraic multiplicity for Part I; emergent-time and observer comparisons for Part II; and pure-gauge benchmarks or cosmological calibration for Part III. Evidence tags describe RAP’s use of a source, not a universal grade assigned to that source’s research.

Entries marked † are supplementary sources from the supplied comparison list, not entries in the corrected manuscript bibliography used for this update. Their inclusion here does not silently amend the PDF or certify their scientific claims. The remaining entries correspond to intended manuscript references, with metadata corrected where identified; this guide is not a new full-text audit of the complete bibliography.

## Finite Algebras, Real Spectral Triples, and the Spectral Standard Model

- [Krajewski (1998)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9701081) — *Classification of finite spectral triples*. Finite-triple classification and multiplicity data; not a RAP selection mechanism.
- [Paschke and Sitarz (1998)](https://arxiv.org/abs/q-alg/9612029) — *Discrete spectral triples and their symmetries*. Real-structure and representation-theoretic foundations.
- [Ćaćić (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-8348-9831-9_2) — *Moduli spaces of Dirac operators for finite spectral triples*. Multiplicity matrices and moduli; classification does not fix RAP input data.
- [Chamseddine and Connes (1997)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9606001) — *The Spectral Action Principle*. Spectral-action comparison under its stated geometric assumptions.
- [Chamseddine, Connes, and Marcolli (2007)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0610241) — *Gravity and the standard model with neutrino mixing*. Almost-commutative Standard-Model construction; not derivation from the RAP kernel.
- [Chamseddine, Connes, and van Suijlekom (2013)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.8050) — *Beyond the Spectral Standard Model: Emergence of Pati–Salam Unification*. Comparison of admissible algebraic routes and their assumptions.
- [Chamseddine and van Suijlekom (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.12392) — *A survey of spectral models of gravity coupled to matter*. Survey context; retain both authors and do not invent a theorem citation.
- [Iochum, Schücker, and Stephan (2004)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0312276) — *On a classification of irreducible almost commutative geometries*. Classification constraints; not a derivation of the general H1/H2 filters.
## Division Algebras and the Connes-Vacuum Attractor Programme

- [Furey (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00612) — *SU(3)C × SU(2)L × U(1)Y (× U(1)X) as a symmetry of division algebraic ladder operators*. Representation and stabilizer comparison with selected algebraic data.
- [Furey and Hughes (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10126) — *Division algebraic symmetry breaking*. Symmetry breaking within an adopted algebraic framework.
- [Gourlay and Gresnigt (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01580) — *Algebraic realisation of three fermion generations with S3 family and unbroken gauge symmetry from Cℓ(8)*. Generation-structure comparison; not endogenous generation selection by RAP.
- [Ishibashi, Kawai, Kitazawa, and Tsuchiya (1997)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9612115) — *A large-N reduced model as superstring*. Matrix-model comparison; no transfer of its results to RAP without a bridge.
- [Azuma, Bal, Nagao, and Nishimura (2004)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0401038) — *Nonperturbative studies of fuzzy spheres in a matrix model with the Chern–Simons term*. Model-specific fuzzy-sphere backgrounds and dynamics.
- [Barrett and Glaser (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.01377) — *Monte Carlo simulations of random non-commutative geometries*. Random-geometry simulations; not proof of the proposed Connes-vacuum attractor.
## Pure Gauge, Lattice, and Topology

- [Athenodorou and Teper (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.06422) — *The glueball spectrum of SU(3) gauge theory in 3+1 dimension*. Pure-SU(3) glueball benchmark context; retain L12 operator separation.
- [Athenodorou and Teper (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00364) — *SU(N) gauge theories in 3+1 dimensions: glueball spectrum, string tensions and topology*. Spectrum, string tension, and topology comparisons.
- [Morningstar and Peardon (1999)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9901004) — *The glueball spectrum from an anisotropic lattice study*. Anisotropic-lattice spectrum reference.
- [Lüscher (2010)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.4518) — *Properties and uses of the Wilson flow in lattice QCD*. Flow and scale-setting methodology.
- [Dürr and Fuwa (2026; preprint 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08217) — *Topological susceptibility and excess kurtosis in SU(3) Yang–Mills theory*. Topology benchmark; specify flow, volume, continuum limit, and scale conventions.
- [Bonanno, Nada, and Vadacchino (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00620) † — *Topological susceptibility of SU(3) pure-gauge theory from out-of-equilibrium simulations*. Preliminary LATTICE2024 methodological comparison, not a completed RAP validation.
- [Morningstar (LATTICE2024; preprint 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02547) — *Update on Glueballs*. Spectroscopy context; distinguish pure-gauge masses from full-QCD state composition.
- [Chernodub et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00376) — *Boundary states and non-Abelian Casimir effect in lattice Yang–Mills theory*. Non-Abelian boundary/Casimir comparison for L15.
- [Ngwenya, Rothkopf, and Horowitz (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21333) — *The Non-Abelian Casimir Effect for Plates, Symmetrical Tube and Box on the Lattice*. Geometry-dependent non-Abelian Casimir comparison; no derivation of the retained 0.66 nm scale.
## Alternative Standard-Model-Origin and Discrete-Emergence Comparisons

- [Levin and Wen (2005)](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0404617) — *String-net condensation: A physical mechanism for topological phases*. String-net/topological-order comparison within its model assumptions.
- [Walker and Wang (2012)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.2632) — *(3+1)-TQFTs and topological insulators*. Topological-model comparison, not a complete Standard-Model derivation.
- [Brower et al. (2004)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nuclphysb.2004.06.007) — *D-theory: field quantization by dimensional reduction of discrete variables*. Quantum-link/D-theory comparison; does not supply RAP’s continuum limit.
- [Nielsen and Ninomiya (1981)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(81)91026-1) † — *A no-go theorem for regularizing chiral fermions*. Companion 1981 no-go paper; the manuscript lists the related Absence of neutrinos papers. Its hypotheses do not exclude every chiral construction.
## Emergent Gravity, Relational Time, and Cosmology Boundaries

- [Jacobson (1995)](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004) — *Thermodynamics of Spacetime: The Einstein Equation of State*. Thermodynamic-gravity comparison with its assumed local spacetime structure.
- [Padmanabhan (2010)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.5004) — *Thermodynamical Aspects of Gravity: New insights*. Thermodynamic aspects of gravity; no automatic kernel-to-metric bridge.
- [Verlinde (2011)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0785) — *On the Origin of Gravity and the Laws of Newton*. Entropic-gravity comparison, not independent confirmation of RAP.
- [Van Raamsdonk (2010)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3035) — *Building up spacetime with quantum entanglement*. Holographic/AdS-CFT context, not a general finite-kernel emergence theorem.
- [Connes and Rovelli (1994)](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9406019) — *Von Neumann algebra automorphisms and time–thermodynamics relation in generally covariant quantum theories*. Thermal-time comparison; choice of algebra and state remains part of the assumptions.
- [Parker et al. (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.08657) — *A Universal Operator Growth Hypothesis*. Operator-growth comparison under stated limits; no theorem of emergent spacetime or lost state-space differentiability.
- [Chishtie (2025; v5, 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18524v5) † — *Emergent spacetime from spatial energy potentiality: a new theoretical framework for early universe cosmology*. Supplementary preprint comparison; its unification and observational claims are not adopted as established results.
- [Sumaya-Martinez (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03958) † — *Fisher-Informational Time: A Causal-Geometric Framework for Emergent Clock Time Physical Distinguishability*. Supplementary emergent-clock comparison; no evidence upgrade for RAP thermal-time or signature claims.
- [Planck Collaboration (2018 results; publication 2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209) — *Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters*. External cosmology input; RAP use remains calibration-only [C].
- [DESI Collaboration (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14738) — *DESI DR2 Results II: Measurements of Baryon Acoustic Oscillations and Cosmological Constraints*. Combination-dependent cosmological constraints; no tension-resolution claim.
- [Riess et al. / SH0ES (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04510) — *A Comprehensive Measurement of the Local Value of the Hubble Constant*. Distance-ladder comparison; a calibration input, not a RAP prediction.
- [KiDS Collaboration / Wright et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19441) — *KiDS-Legacy: Cosmological constraints from cosmic shear with the complete Kilo-Degree Survey*. Survey-specific weak-lensing context.
- [DES Collaboration (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14559) — *Dark Energy Survey Year 6 Results: Cosmological Constraints from Galaxy Clustering and Weak Lensing*. Survey- and pipeline-specific clustering/lensing context; RAP use capped at [C].
## Structural Realism and Information Ontology

- [Ladyman and Ross, with Spurrett and Collier (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199276196.001.0001) — *Every Thing Must Go: Metaphysics Naturalized*. Structural-realist framing; philosophical alignment is not empirical validation.
- [Floridi (2008; online 2007)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-007-9163-z) † — *A defence of informational structural realism*. Supplementary informational-ontology comparison, not a RAP mathematical premise.
- [Le Nepvou (2025)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16962526) † — *Structural Realism Beyond Static Form: Toward a Dynamically Grounded Ontology*. Supplementary preprint on dynamical ontology; metadata availability is not endorsement of its argument.
## Observer and Consciousness Boundary

- [Friston (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2787) — *The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?*. Neuroscientific comparison; does not establish the RAP observer functor.
- [Metzinger (2003)](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262633086/being-no-one/) — *Being No One: The Self-Model Theory of Subjectivity*. Self-model comparison matching the manuscript’s cited book; not a physics derivation.
- [Tononi (2004)](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2202-5-42) — *An information integration theory of consciousness*. Consciousness-theory comparison; RAP’s use remains interpretive [E].
- [Hoffman and Prakash (2014)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00577) — *Objects of Consciousness*. The 2014 work actually cited in the manuscript; interpretive contrast only.
- [Hoffman, Singh, and Prakash (2015)](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-015-0890-8) † — *The Interface Theory of Perception*. Supplementary perception comparison, distinct from Objects of Consciousness (2014).
- [Hameroff and Penrose (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2013.08.002) — *Consciousness in the universe: A review of the Orch OR theory*. Primary Orch-OR comparison; no claimed RAP consciousness mechanism or MeV-to-neural bridge.
