# Evidence system and subtypes

The programme retains A, A−, B, C, D and E as its manuscript's classification grammar. These labels are not a replacement for method, source, uncertainty, scope or independent replication.

- **A: formal mathematics or exact arithmetic** — a conclusion under stated assumptions, or an exact identity. It is not automatically a statement about nature.
- **A: definition/input** — a declared convention, adopted value, or record structure. Exact adoption is not experimental exactness.
- **A: policy/document-state** — a governance boundary or an observable state of the document. A policy is not a physical theorem.
- **A: reported software** — preserve the named commit and declaration; distinguish a historical report from an independently executed build.
- **A−: calibrated** — in particular the carried gamma value. Do not describe calibration as derivation.
- **B: empirical reference/external result** — retain the specific source and uncertainty model. Incomplete source audits remain explicitly pending; a B tag does not close them.
- **C: calibration/comparison** — cosmological uses remain capped here; no observational tension is declared resolved.
- **D: open programme or physical bridge** — includes physical H1/H2 selection, continuum emergence and unconstructed gauge/observer bridges.
- **E: interpretation or historical material** — no import as an active physical premise. The subatomic torsion reference remains E under D22.

`LEDGER/CLAIMS.json` therefore has separate `evidence_class`, `evidence_kind`, `status`, `source`, `method` and `scope` fields. PASS in a software report does not upgrade any of them. No automatic confidence score, sigma, external signature, or PI override is manufactured.
