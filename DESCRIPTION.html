Evidence Grammar and Strata

Six classes are used: [A] for mathematical closure under stated assumptions, exact identities, or explicitly typed deterministic, documentary, and policy statements; [A−] for calibrated internal closure, not derivation; [B] for benchmark-related physics at its stated source and uncertainty status; [C] for the cosmology cap; [D] for a falsifiable research programme; and [E] for interpretive, speculative, withdrawn, or museum content. A policy, an input definition, and a proof are not interchangeable merely because the ledger uses the same tag.

A residual below 10⁻¹⁴ at 80-digit working precision is an observed computation, not by itself an exact certificate or rigorous error bound. Exact arithmetic, certified enclosures, or an explicit error analysis is needed for the stronger claim. Stratum I contains empirical inputs; Stratum II contains established theory under its assumptions; Stratum III contains RAP-specific hypotheses and interpretation. No Stratum-III mapping becomes empirical evidence by analogy.
