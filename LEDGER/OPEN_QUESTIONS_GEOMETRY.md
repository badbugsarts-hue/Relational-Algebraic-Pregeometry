- Derive H1/H2 physically, or replace them with a target-independent selector; N=6 remains a declared input.
- Construct the missing representation, real structure, multiplicities, chirality and spectral data rather than treating a block signature as a spectral triple.
- Supply dynamics and a controlled large-N/coarse-grained limit; finite enumeration is not continuum emergence.
- Explain the origin and signature of spacetime without importing the historical background metric as a derived result.
- Construct operator/correlator matching for any proposed Yang–Mills observable.
- Supply an independently auditable Born-rule/observer bridge if such a claim is pursued.

These are research questions, not universal no-go theorems. See manuscript limitations L8–L15 and the source-relative G1–G4 discussion.
