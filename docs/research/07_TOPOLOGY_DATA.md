# Topology benchmarks require source-clean data

**Status:** new RAP v4.0 working note, 27 October 2026; no PI approval or evidence upgrade.

The package contains no new lattice ensemble, HMC chain, continuum extrapolation or topological-charge measurement. Comparisons remain pending their source and operator audit.

A usable topology benchmark needs the operator definition, flow/smoothing prescription, lattice spacings, volume and continuum procedure, units and scale-setting, uncertainty and covariance. If a number is quoted in MeV for the fourth root of susceptibility, it must not be labelled as susceptibility itself.

No inherited “0.37 sigma” output is adopted as evidence. FSS/P2 remains under the recorded D24 quarantine because the required raw run artefacts are not supplied.
