# Finite algebra signature versus spectral triple

**Status:** new RAP v4.0 working note, 27 October 2026; no PI approval or evidence upgrade.

For complex matrix blocks of sizes n_i, the bookkeeping signature has complex algebra dimension sum(n_i^2), centre dimension equal to the number of summands, and off-diagonal count sum(n_i(n_i-1)). For [3,2,1] these are 14, 3 and 8.

The engine explicitly marks physical representation, real structure and spectral-triple construction as absent. A complex block list does not determine a quaternionic real form, a Hilbert-space representation, chirality, bimodule multiplicity or a Dirac operator. The structural map must not be described as an equivalence with a full classification or a construction of the Standard-Model algebra.

See manuscript L11 and the source-relative G1–G4 discussion. No new categorical or physical completeness theorem is offered.
