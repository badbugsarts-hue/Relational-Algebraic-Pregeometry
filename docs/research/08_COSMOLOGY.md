# Calibration is not tension resolution

**Status:** new RAP v4.0 working note, 27 October 2026; no PI approval or evidence upgrade.

Cosmological entries remain C-class comparisons or calibrations. External data releases and pipeline-dependent constraints must keep their own provenance. No pregeometric derivation of dark energy, a Hubble value or a resolved observational tension is supplied here.

The historical scalar/holographic suppression expressions and their fitted factors are not reinstated as active equations by this repository. A new description or numerical report cannot alter the contents of an old deposit. Read the actual final manuscript and the source record for any claimed calibration.
